How do I keep my LEGO® collection in one list?

Choose one list as the one you trust, and let everything else feed it.

The answer

  1. 1
    Choose one list as the one you trustEverything else feeds it, and you stop asking which list is right.
  2. 2
    Record more than setsLoose parts, minifigures and what each copy is missing are what you look up most when you build.
  3. 3
    Bring your lists over instead of retyping themMost collection sites and apps export a file, and some can be read directly.
  4. 4
    Record each copy on its ownTwo copies of the same set are rarely in the same state.
  5. 5
    Keep it where you can take it with youWith an export or a backup you control.
Sync needs your own accountsSo the demo shows the list, not the sync.

How Brick Work does it

Brick Work keeps your sets, each copy on its own, plus minifigures, loose parts, part lots, instructions and boxes in one collection on your own machine.

  • Sync with Rebrickable and Brickset. Pull your sets and wishlist in, with a preview row by row. A pull never deletes anything in Brick Work. Sending back shows every change first, and removals stay unticked until you tick them. It needs the site's API key and your own account login. Read the sync guide.
  • Lost Parts from Rebrickable: a pull can mark the parts you listed as lost there as missing on the matching copy.
  • Import files: a BrickLink wanted list or store inventory (XML), a Brickset CSV, a Rebrickable CSV, or BrickTracker's database.
  • Every import shows a preview before anything is added, and can be undone.
