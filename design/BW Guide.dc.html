Guide
General guidance for every page: principles, part colours, tokens, navigation, data-type language, shell, icons and devices. The four nav pages show each workflow in all sizes.
G1Principles
Brick Work is a quiet workbench for one person and one collection. It sits inside Toni's World and shares its tokens: paper-and-ink surfaces, 0.5 px hairlines, no shadows, no gradients, no emoji. Clay is the only interaction colour.
Direction
·Reduced, usability first.·One primary action per screen.·Data types are told apart by form, not by colour (see 1a).·Hichert SUCCESS where it carries meaning: found, complete, difference.
Voice
·Warm, short, sentence case.·Bare counts, mid-dots, no exclamation marks.·Names: Part to Inventory, Check Set Parts, Tools & Admin, Wishlist, "Set 1" for a copy.·English UI.
Never
·The word LEGO, its logo or red and yellow.·A second interaction colour, gradients, shadows, emoji, a mascot.·A colour without its name, or a bare colour dot.·Brand accent (teal) on controls, data, charts or status.
Devices
·Main: 2020 MacBook, 1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800.·Sometimes: iPad mini 5, 768 × 1024 and 1024 × 768.·Very rarely: iPhone 16, 390.·Every workflow shows all sizes in one row on its page.
G2Colour guidance · part colours
A part colour is a swatch filled with that colour, with its name inside. Everywhere, in the same form, in both themes.
Source
·Rebrickable colour catalogue (colors.csv): id, name, rgb, is_trans. Sample data: test-data/colors.json (43 colours).·Join on the Rebrickable colour id. BrickLink and LDraw names differ; always show the Rebrickable name.·Do not hard-code colours in screens. Draw from the catalogue; refresh it with the catalogue sync.
Fill and ink
·Fill = the colour's rgb.·Ink = #1A1816 when relative luminance is above 0.45, otherwise paper #FAFAF8. Same rule in light and dark.·Transparent colours: fill at 55 % over the page ground, name in dark ink, never a checkerboard.·Unknown id: name "Unknown colour", neutral --tw-surface-2 fill, raw id in mono. Never guess a colour.
Order and use
·Colour first, part number second: "Dark Brown · 3022".·Colour is identity, not status. Status uses the status pills only.·Required colour on a part screen is a tile at least 96 px tall; lists use the 22 px inline chip; choosing uses 56 px chips.
