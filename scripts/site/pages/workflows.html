Nine ways Brick Work helps at the table.

Six workflows on the Workbench, and three more that help you decide what to build. Four are walked through step by step; the rest have a short guide.

Part Spotter

Photograph a piece or figure and name it. Photos queue up while you work; you confirm each result.

  1. 1
    Take a photoWith the camera, or add a saved one. Choose Parts or Figures.
  2. 2
    Check the best matchThe reference part is shown large, your photo beside it. Other possibilities are one tap away.
  3. 3
    Queue what you confirmApproved pieces go to a verified queue.
  4. 4
    Add the batchWhen a lot is done, confirm once and add them to loose parts or a lot.

Set Inspector

Go through a copy part by part against its inventory and keep a running count of what is missing.

  1. 1
    Pick a copyUnchecked copies, largest first. A check in progress stays on top.
  2. 2
    Choose an orderLargest first, inventory order, by colour or by category group. Spare parts are optional.
  3. 3
    Count each partFound, Missing or Skip for later. The count stays on screen; Undo is one tap.
  4. 4
    See the gapsMissing parts per copy, ready to export as BrickLink XML.
  5. 5
    Pick up laterStop any time. The check resumes where you left it.

Part Runner

Tick several sets and get the missing parts you can pull from your sorted loose stock, in your own order.

  1. 1
    Choose the setsAll incomplete sets, or only the ones you tick.
  2. 2
    Sort like your drawersOrder the pick list the way your loose stock is sorted.
  3. 3
    Pull each partPart, colour and how many to pick, with the copies it goes to.
  4. 4
    Decide where it goesInto a set, back to loose parts, or onto the pick list only.

Matchmaker

See which incomplete copies your loose stock can finish, then decide what to build or buy.

  1. 1
    See what stock can finishCopies your loose parts complete come first, then by coverage.
  2. 2
    Open the copyLoose stock finds, set detail takes: move the parts into the copy there.

Orders

Count what arrived and allocate it to the copies that need it.

Import a placed order from BrickLink or paste the order email, count what came out of the bag, and put each piece where it belongs.

Read the guide

Storage

Say which drawer or box holds each part, and find it again.

Name any place (a box, a drawer, a shelf, a room); places can sit inside each other. Anything without one shows as Not stored.

Read the guide

Build Scout

Rank every set by how much of it your loose stock already makes, with the cost of the rest.

Each row shows the percentage your stock covers, the missing parts and figures and an approximate cost. Narrow by stock source, minimum, theme, years and size.

Read the guide

Build it

Turn a set into a copy as one undoable step.

Take the best set from Build Scout, turn it into a copy and collect the missing parts of several sets into one BrickLink wanted list.

Read the guide

Set detection

Find the sets inside a bulk lot.

Brick Work ranks the sets a lot may hold from its distinctive parts (parts that 25 sets or fewer have), with the reasons and what is still missing.
