Lost Parts

InBothOutOff

Marks the parts on your Lost Parts list as missing on the matching copy, and sends the parts you find or newly miss here back.

Into Brick Work: which count wins
Add only
The sync only adds missing marks. Where both sides have a count, the larger is kept. Nothing is cleared.
Rebrickable wins per part
For a part Rebrickable lists as lost, Rebrickable's count replaces yours, also when it is smaller. Marks on other parts stay.
Rebrickable wins per set
A set with at least one Lost Part on Rebrickable becomes exactly Rebrickable's list. Other missing marks on that copy are cleared.
Rebrickable wins everywhere
Every synced set becomes exactly Rebrickable's list. A set with no Lost Parts there loses its missing marks.
To Rebrickable

Parts newly missing here are added to Lost Parts there; parts found here are removed there. Removals are unticked until you tick them.
