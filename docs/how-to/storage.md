# Know where things are

*Since 0.5.0.*

Do this when you put things away or reorganise: record which box, drawer or shelf holds each set, minifigure, part lot or box, so you can find it again.

A storage is any place you name: a box, a drawer, a shelf, a room. Storages can sit inside each other. Anything without a storage is **Not stored**.

## Add a storage

1. Open **Workbench → Storage**.
2. Press **Add storage**.
3. Enter a **Name**, for example *Drawer 14*. Choose a **Type** (Box, Shelf, Drawer, Bin, Bag, Case, Cabinet, Room or Other) and, if it sits in another storage, choose that under **Inside**. Add a **Note** if you like.
4. Press **Add storage**.

To change a storage later, select it in the list and press **Edit**, change the fields and press **Save**.

## Give one thing a place

1. Open the item's page from **Collection**: a set's [copy](../concepts/collection-model.md), a minifigure, a part, an instruction or a box.
2. Press **Edit**.
3. Choose its **Storage**, or **Not stored**.
4. Press **Save**.

The page's facts then show where it is. A part [lot](../concepts/collection-model.md) has its own page; editing its storage applies to the whole lot.

## Give many things a place

On any **Collection** list:

1. Turn on **Select mode**.
2. Tick the items.
3. In the bar that appears, under **Change**, choose **Storage**, then the storage under **Value**.
4. Press **Save**.

When you put selected loose stock into a new lot, the **Put selected stock in a lot** sheet also asks for a **Storage**.

## Move things between storages

1. Open **Workbench → Storage** and select the storage that holds them.
2. Press **Select** and tick the sets, minifigures, part lots or other items to move.
3. Press **Move to another storage**.
4. Under **Move to**, choose the new storage, or **Not stored**.
5. Press **Move selected items**.

A lot takes its parts with it: loose parts filed in that lot and in the same storage move too. Up to 1,000 items move at a time.

## See what's in a storage

Select a storage in the list on **Workbench → Storage**. It shows its type, where it sits, its note, and its contents grouped as **Sets**, **Minifigures**, **Part lots** and **Other** (instructions, boxes and the like), with a search and filters. **Not stored** in the same list shows everything without a place.

On the **Collection** lists you can also filter by **Storage** or group by it.

## Archive a storage

1. Select the storage and press **Edit**.
2. Open **Delete storage**. It shows how many copies, loose entries, pieces and lots it holds.
3. Move the items elsewhere first, or tick **Move everything to Not stored, including lots**.
4. Press **Archive storage**.

The storage disappears from lists and pickers. You can undo it in History.

## History and Undo

Open **History** at the bottom of **Workbench → Storage**. It lists storages you added or changed, moves and archives made on that page, newest first, each with an **Undo** while it can still be undone.

## Purchase locations

Where things came from lives next door. On **Workbench → Storage**, follow **Purchase locations**.

- The list shows each place with its **Kind**, the **Orders** whose store has the same name, the number of **Copies** bought there, when it was **Last used** and what you **Spent** (from the price paid, per currency).
- Choose a **Kind** (BrickLink store, Online shop, Shop, Market, Fair, Private or Other) and it saves at once.
- Press a place's name to see the sets bought there.
- **Add location** opens **Tools & Admin**, where purchase places are kept.

To record where a copy came from, open it, press **Edit** and choose its **Purchase location**.
