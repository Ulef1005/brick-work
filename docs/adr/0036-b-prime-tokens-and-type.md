# 0036. B′ tokens and type: the layer takes the frames' values, IBM Plex is served

- **Status:** accepted; amends [0022](0022-design-roll-out.md) (its "no visual change" premise for the token layer ends here)
- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Decided by:** Ulf (direction B′ Drawer Tag, its frames and W-Q7, design/APPROVALS.md, 19 November 2026); brick-work-design and brick-work-product for the build choices below (19 November 2026, issue #492)

## Context

ADR 0022 moved Brick Work onto the design builder's neutral base with a layer that served the old values unchanged. Milestone U rebuilds the app to the approved B′ direction. The frames in `design/screens-b/` are generated by `design/sources/b/b_frames.py`, which holds the B′ token values (`TOKENS`, `DARK`). Screens are rebuilt one issue at a time, so for a while rebuilt and unrebuilt screens are served side by side from the same tokens.

## Decision

- **The token layer takes the B′ values from the frames' source.** `design/tokens/brickwork-tokens.css` carries every role of `TOKENS` (light) and `DARK` (dark) in all four theme blocks, field, tile and tile ink included. `tests/test_design_tokens.py` reads `b_frames.py` with `ast` and fails on any difference, so a value changes in the frames first. Focus is the ink ring in both themes, never violet.
- **Tokens that unrebuilt screens still need stay defined.** Success, warning and info keep their shipped values until the last screen that uses them is rebuilt (DESIGN-CHECK § Until a screen is rebuilt). Text-faint takes the muted value, divider-strong the B′ hairline, the sheet the frames' ground (`--tw-bg`), danger-soft its shipped tint. The viz ramp is ink. The type scale is unchanged: rebuilt screens take their sizes from the frame CSS.
- **Text on the accent is `--tw-on-accent`.** Every rule that had ink pinned on an accent ground (`--bw-action-ink` and 16 literal `#1A1816`s, right for clay, wrong for violet) uses the token: white on violet in light, ink in dark.
- **IBM Plex is served as a brand asset.** `design/fonts/` is copied to `static/brickwork/fonts/` by `scripts/brand_assets.py`, declared in `toni.css` and precached by the service worker; the docs site takes Plex Sans and Mono through `scripts/docs_theme.py`. Inter and Geist Mono stay on disk for the printed labels (approved as drawn, L01) and the website. The website's identity lines in `brick-work.css` (its teal accent and the Patrick Hand wordmark) stay until the website moves to B′ (#526).
- **The install colours are the app bar surface** (W-Q7): the manifest, the `?theme=dark` manifest and the theme-color metas take `--tw-surface`, `#F8F8F6` in light and `#1F2322` in dark, pinned to the layer by a test.

## Consequences

- Unrebuilt screens change colour, type face, radii and hairlines now and keep their layout. Their status dots and bars keep their colours until their own issue rebuilds them. Statistics' bars stay violet until T8 (#501).
- The website build takes the B′ colours from `tokens-v2.css` while it keeps its own fonts and accent, so it looks mixed until #526. Pages deploys on a release tag only, so the live site shows this only if a release ships before #526.
- The shell, the lockup, the X device and the per-copy bar follow in the next step: their CSS is generated from `b_frames.py`'s `B_COMPONENTS` (#537), scoped to rebuilt screens, and the app-vs-frame check renders the approved frame HTML and the app page in the same pinned browser.
