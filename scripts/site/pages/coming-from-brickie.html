Coming from Brickie.

Brickie, the Brickset companion for iPhone, was removed from the App Store. It saved the sets you own and want to your Brickset account, so they are still there, and Brick Work, a free, open-source alternative, can bring them over.

SetsWanted setsMinifigures by CSVPreview before anything is added

Move your Brickie sets to Brick Work

  1. 1
    Check your Brickset accountThe sets you owned and wanted in Brickie are under My Sets on brickset.com, with the account you signed in with in the app.
  2. 2
    Sync your accountCollection sync reads your owned and wanted sets with a Brickset API key and your Brickset sign-in, row by row in a preview.
  3. 3
    Or use the CSV filesExport My Sets from Brickset and choose a CSV file; Brickset's minifigure export brings the figures you own loose.
  4. 4
    Check what came inIn your collection counts the copies, loose entries and wishes that came in.
