<section class="walkthrough check-set-parts" id="check-set-parts" data-walkthrough><div><p class="proof">01 · 142 of 380</p><h2>Check Set Parts</h2><p>Go through a copy part by part against its inventory and keep a running count of what is missing.</p><ol class="steps">
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Pick a copy</h3><p>Unchecked copies, largest first. A check in progress stays on top.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Choose an order</h3><p>Largest first, inventory order or by colour. Spare parts are optional.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Count each part</h3><p>Found, Missing or Skip for later. The count stays on screen; Undo is one tap.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>See the gaps</h3><p>Missing parts per copy, ready to export as BrickLink XML.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Pick up later</h3><p>Stop any time. The check resumes where you left it.</p></div></li>
</ol>

<section class="walkthrough part-picker" id="part-picker" data-walkthrough><div><p class="proof">02 · pick 4</p><h2>Part Picker</h2><p>Tick several sets and get the missing parts you can pull from your sorted loose stock, in your own order.</p><ol class="steps">
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Choose the sets</h3><p>All incomplete sets, or only the ones you tick.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Sort like your drawers</h3><p>Order the pick list the way your loose stock is sorted.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Pull each part</h3><p>Part, colour and how many to pick, with the copies it goes to.</p></div></li>
<li><div class="step-copy"><h3>Decide where it goes</h3><p>Into a set, back to loose parts, or onto the pick list only.</p></div></li>
</ol>
