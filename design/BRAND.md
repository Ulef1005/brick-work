# Brick Work brand guide

Brick Work has its own design, outside Toni's World (Ulf, 2026-11-03; ADR 0022). **Direction: B′ Drawer Tag** (approved 2026-11-19): the app is labelled like your drawers. Storage groups sit under violet label tape, found parts are violet, status is told by shape (filled, hatched, dashed) and a word, and the logo's X marks a set or copy not checked yet. Violet `#7334CF` is the one accent, for identity and action; ink and a pale grey ground carry the rest (Ulf: "B it is", "Retire clay", "Yes, X = not checked", "Caps on tape and tabs only"). This guide follows the App Design Standard's order (P1). It is being filled in during the roll-out (#158 D1 → #161 D4): until a section is approved under the standard, it names where the current, previously approved design lives and reads **Not yet approved under the standard**. Approvals are recorded in [APPROVALS.md](APPROVALS.md). The rules for building with it are the `brickwork-design` skill (`.claude/skills/brickwork-design/SKILL.md`).

Hichert's SUCCESS governs this design. The per-rule lines are written when the brand guide is approved (workflow step 7). Not yet approved under the standard.

## 1. Logo

**The mark (approved, W1 of logo round 4).** Three stud-less plates laid in a bond, one over two, all one unit placed only by transforms, in Violet `#7334CF`. A white X is cut out of the top plate's upper-right corner (a mask, not a painted X): it marks a new brick and always shows the ground behind it. The wordmark is "brick work", lowercase, in Outfit SemiBold (SIL OFL 1.1), set as outlines in ink `#111214`, to the right of the mark at 56 units on the mark's 100-unit box.

**Variants (logo round 5).** Every file is generated by `sources/logo/brand_logo.py` from one source; never edit one by hand.

| Variant | File in `logo/` | Use |
|---|---|---|
| LG01 primary lockup, on light | `lockup-horizontal.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-light.png` | Default wherever the name is shown |
| LG02 mark alone, on light | `mark.svg` | Where the name is already on screen |
| LG03 on dark grounds | `lockup-horizontal-dark.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-dark.png`, `mark-dark.svg` | Mark `#B995F6`, name `#EDEDEF` |
| LG04 on the brand field | `lockup-brand-field.svg` | All white on violet: headers, splash, slides |
| LG05 one colour, ink | `lockup-mono-ink.svg`, `mark-line.svg` | One-colour print and labels |
| LG06 one colour, white | `lockup-mono-white.svg` | Photos and dark prints where violet does not hold |
| LG07 small mark | `mark-small.svg`, `mark-small-dark.svg`, `../app-icon/favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-24.png` | Below 32 px the X is dropped (the app bar's 24 px mark, tab icons) |

**Clear space:** one top-plate height (26.3 units on the 100-unit box) on every side of the visible plates. **Minimum size:** the mark with the X from 32 px; below that the small mark without the X; the lockup from 120 px wide. **Misuse:** never recolour the plates outside the variants, add studs, move, enlarge or recolour the X, paint the X on instead of cutting it, rotate or stretch the mark, set the name in another face or case, or place the light lockup on a ground that fails contrast.

**SUCCESS.** Say: one idea, brick work laid as a bond, with the X for a new brick. Unify: one mark everywhere; variants differ only in what the ground demands. Condense: no detail lost at 16 px, because the small mark drops the X there. Check: clear space and minimum size are set, the mark is never distorted, every variant passes contrast on its ground. Express: the form carries the app's idea and its X. Simplify: three plates and a cut, no flourish. Structure: one lockup structure, mark then name.

Approved: the mark and lockup in round 4, the variants, clear space, minimum sizes and misuse in round 5 (review artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LVHh16j6JvBvvLz7YawPoA, versions 4 and 5). The cross-stitch mark it replaces is kept in `logo/retired/`, never served.

## 2. App logo

The white mark on a violet tile (`#7334CF`, corner radius 22.5 % of the side, mark at 66 %), generated by `sources/logo/brand_logo.py` into `app-icon/`: `app-icon.svg` (any), `icon-maskable.svg` (full bleed, mark at 52 % inside the 80 % safe zone), `icons/icon-{120,152,167,180,192,512,1024}.png`, `icons/icon-maskable-{192,512,1024}.png`, favicons (`favicon.svg` and 16 and 24 px without the X, 32 and 48 px with it, `favicon.ico` with 16, 32 and 48), and the splash screens in `splash/` (the mark on paper `#FAFAF8`, or in `#B995F6` on `#111214`, its box 30 % of the screen's short side). `sources/logo/manifest-snippet.json` gives the icon list and colours for the app's own `static/brickwork/manifest.webmanifest`. `head-block.html` keeps the app's head tags. The cross-stitch icon set is in `app-icon/retired/`, never served.

Approved in logo round 5 (review artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LVHh16j6JvBvvLz7YawPoA, version 5). The device-sized splash screens and the 48 px favicon are derived from the approved 512 px splash and favicons, not separately approved.

## 3. Colour

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-03): ground `#EDEEEB`, surface `#F8F8F6`, surface-2 `#E3E5E1`, divider `#C9CDC8`, ink `#1A1E1E`, secondary `#434A4A`, muted `#59615F` (5.5:1 on the ground), accent violet `#7334CF` with soft `#F0EAFB` (identity, action, links, selected), on-accent white (6.7:1), focus ink, danger `#8B4F54` (errors only, always with a word). Success, warning and info have no colours: status is shape and a word. Every colour is checked against the 30 most-used LEGO part colours (ΔE OKLab under 0.10 and hue within 20° is rejected). The values are listed beside the shipped ones in the review (R2-03); `tokens/brickwork-tokens.css` takes them when the app is rebuilt in milestone U, and until then serves the values from before the redesign. Dark mode is derived from these roles.

## 4. Type

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-03): IBM Plex Sans for text, IBM Plex Sans Condensed in capitals for tape, tabs and small section labels only, IBM Plex Mono for numbers, codes and set numbers. Set names and headings are sentence case. The files and their licence are in `fonts/` (`fonts/README.md`). The app serves Inter, Geist Mono and Patrick Hand until it is rebuilt in milestone U.

## 5. Layout

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-01): four tabs across the top (Collection, Workbench, Planning, Manage), the open group's pages in a row under them; on the phone the same four groups in the tab bar and the group's pages as a scrolling row. The lockup at 32 px leads the app bar. Radii 3, 4 and 8 px; 1 px hairlines and a 1.5 px ink rule under headers, tiles and the app bar; 44 px targets. Frames: `screens-b/`.

## 6. Components

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-04): primary violet and secondary ink-outline buttons, the ink focus ring (2 px, 2 px offset), tabs, label tape, tags and dashed tags, the X chip for "not checked" (the logo's X, white on a violet square; a thin line X without a square means close or stop), progress per copy to scale, one cell per part, fields. Sheet: `screens-b/00-components/`.
