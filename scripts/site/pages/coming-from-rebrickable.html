Coming from Rebrickable or Brickset.

Bring your sets and wishlist over without typing them in again. Every way in shows a preview before anything is added, and an import can be undone.

SetsWishlistPreview before anything is added

How to bring it in

  1. 1
    Open Get startedThe empty Sets page, or Tools & Admin › Get started, step 3.
  2. 2
    Sync your accountCollection sync reads your sets and wishlist with the site's API key, row by row in a preview.
  3. 3
    Or use a Brickset CSVExport My Sets from Brickset and choose a CSV file; Owned takes the sets you own, Wishlist the ones you want.
  4. 4
    Check what came inIn your collection counts the copies, loose entries and wishes, tagged imported from Rebrickable or Brickset.
