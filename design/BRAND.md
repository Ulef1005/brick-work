# Brick Work brand guide

Brick Work has its own design, outside Toni's World (Ulf, 2026-11-03; ADR 0022). **Direction: B′ Drawer Tag** (approved 2026-11-19): the app is labelled like your drawers. Storage groups sit under violet label tape, found parts are violet, status is told by shape (filled, hatched, dashed) and a word, and the logo's X marks a set or copy not checked yet. Violet is the one accent, for identity and action; ink and a pale grey ground carry the rest (Ulf: "B it is", "Retire clay", "Yes, X = not checked", "Caps on tape and tabs only"). This guide follows the App Design Standard's order (P1); every section below is approved, light and dark, and records where its files are and which approval carries it ([APPROVALS.md](APPROVALS.md)). The app is rebuilt to it in milestone U: a screen not rebuilt yet still looks as shipped (the v1.3 frames in `screens/`, the rules in [DESIGN-CHECK.md](DESIGN-CHECK.md) § Until a screen is rebuilt). The rules for building with it are the `brickwork-design` skill (`.claude/skills/brickwork-design/SKILL.md`).

## SUCCESS

Hichert's SUCCESS governs this whole design, not only its charts. How Brick Work meets each rule, in one line each:

- **Say:** every screen states its one message in its heading, often as the answer itself ("3 of 7 sets incomplete", "10 parts missing, 4 in loose stock"); the primary action is the one violet button.
- **Unify:** one bar language everywhere (violet found, hatched missing, dashed still to check), one label tape for every storage group, one status family by shape, one word per thing ("Copy 1", "Workbench · Orders").
- **Condense:** the content fills the screen; chrome is one app bar, one row of pages and 1 px hairlines; numbers sit in mono beside what they count.
- **Check:** bars start at zero and are to scale, percentages come from the same numbers, no minus before a number, every text pair passes 4.5:1 in light and dark, and violet is never used for something that is not found, complete, chosen or an action.
- **Express:** shape for status, tape for a place, cells for parts, a stepper for a count, a switch for on and off, a segment for one of few, tiles where you recognise a thing by its picture.
- **Simplify:** no shadows except sheets and the scrim, no gradients, no icons without a job, no second accent; the X has one meaning.
- **Structure:** each screen in the task's order (the copy, then its parts, then its money; choose, then review); one grid, one column on the phone; tokens grouped by role.

Approval: the step 7 line in APPROVALS.md. Each section below ends with a **SUCCESS** line naming the rules it carries most.

## 1. Logo

**The mark (approved, W1 of logo round 4).** Three stud-less plates laid in a bond, one over two, all one unit placed only by transforms, in Violet `#7334CF`. A white X is cut out of the top plate's upper-right corner (a mask, not a painted X): it marks a new brick and always shows the ground behind it. The wordmark is "brick work", lowercase, in Outfit SemiBold (SIL OFL 1.1), set as outlines in ink `#111214`, to the right of the mark at 56 units on the mark's 100-unit box.

**Variants (logo round 5).** Every file is generated by `sources/logo/brand_logo.py` from one source; never edit one by hand.

| Variant | File in `logo/` | Use |
|---|---|---|
| LG01 primary lockup, on light | `lockup-horizontal.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-light.png` | Default wherever the name is shown |
| LG02 mark alone, on light | `mark.svg` | Where the name is already on screen |
| LG03 on dark grounds | `lockup-horizontal-dark.svg`, `lockup-horizontal-dark.png`, `mark-dark.svg` | Mark `#B995F6`, name `#EDEDEF` |
| LG04 on the brand field | `lockup-brand-field.svg` | All white on violet: headers, splash, slides |
| LG05 one colour, ink | `lockup-mono-ink.svg`, `mark-line.svg` | One-colour print and labels |
| LG06 one colour, white | `lockup-mono-white.svg` | Photos and dark prints where violet does not hold |
| LG07 small mark | `mark-small.svg`, `mark-small-dark.svg`, `../app-icon/favicon.svg`, `favicon-16.png`, `favicon-24.png` | Below 32 px the X is dropped (the app bar's 24 px mark, tab icons) |

**Clear space:** one top-plate height (26.3 units on the 100-unit box) on every side of the visible plates. **Minimum size:** the mark with the X from 32 px; below that the small mark without the X; the lockup from 120 px wide. **Misuse:** never recolour the plates outside the variants, add studs, move, enlarge or recolour the X, paint the X on instead of cutting it, rotate or stretch the mark, set the name in another face or case, or place the light lockup on a ground that fails contrast.

**SUCCESS.** Say: one idea, brick work laid as a bond, with the X for a new brick. Unify: one mark everywhere; variants differ only in what the ground demands. Condense: no detail lost at 16 px, because the small mark drops the X there. Check: clear space and minimum size are set, the mark is never distorted, every variant passes contrast on its ground. Express: the form carries the app's idea and its X. Simplify: three plates and a cut, no flourish. Structure: one lockup structure, mark then name.

Approved: the mark and lockup in round 4, the variants, clear space, minimum sizes and misuse in round 5 (review artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LVHh16j6JvBvvLz7YawPoA, versions 4 and 5). The cross-stitch mark it replaces is kept in `logo/retired/`, never served.

## 2. App logo

The white mark on a violet tile (`#7334CF`, corner radius 22.5 % of the side, mark at 66 %), generated by `sources/logo/brand_logo.py` into `app-icon/`: `app-icon.svg` (any), `icon-maskable.svg` (full bleed, mark at 52 % inside the 80 % safe zone), `icons/icon-{120,152,167,180,192,512,1024}.png`, `icons/icon-maskable-{192,512,1024}.png`, favicons (`favicon.svg` and 16 and 24 px without the X, 32 and 48 px with it, `favicon.ico` with 16, 32 and 48), and the splash screens in `splash/` (the mark on paper `#F8F8F6`, or in `#B995F6` on `#1F2322`, its box 30 % of the screen's short side). `sources/logo/manifest-snippet.json` gives the icon list and colours for the app's own `static/brickwork/manifest.webmanifest`. `head-block.html` keeps the app's head tags. The cross-stitch icon set is in `app-icon/retired/`, never served.

Approved in logo round 5 (review artifact https://claude.ai/artifact/LVHh16j6JvBvvLz7YawPoA, version 5). The device-sized splash screens and the 48 px favicon are derived from the approved 512 px splash and favicons, not separately approved. The splash grounds are the manifest values, light #F8F8F6 and dark #1F2322 (Ulf, via wiki-agent, 2026-11-19: "Align to the manifest (Recommended)"; "Manifest dark #1F2322 (Recommended)").

| Role | Light | Dark | Job |

| `--tw-surface` | `#F8F8F6` | `#1F2322` | cards, bars, the tab bar |
| `--tw-surface-2` | `#E3E5E1` | `#292E2C` | wells, pressed rows |

| `--tw-text` | `#1A1E1E` | `#E3E6E2` | ink; also chart bars (§ 10) |

| `--tw-accent-soft` | `#F0EAFB` | `#2F2547` | selected rows, the X chip, a chosen source |

| `--tw-field` | `#FFFFFF` | `#101312` | fields |
| `--tw-tile` | `#FFFFFF` | `#E4E6E2` | picture tiles |
| `--tw-tile-ink` | `#59615F` | `#4A524F` | alt text on a tile |

Success, warning and info have no colours: status is shape and a word. Every text pair passes 4.5:1 and every UI part 3:1 in both themes. Every colour is checked against the 30 most-used LEGO part colours (ΔE OKLab under 0.10 and hue within 20° is rejected), so a violet bar never reads as a violet part. That rule is why dark has two violets on purpose: the logo keeps its approved on-dark `#B995F6` (LG03), and the accent is `#C080FE`, because `#B995F6` sits too close to LEGO Medium Violet `#9391E4`.

**Web manifest.** Its theme and background colour is the app bar surface `--tw-surface`, `#F8F8F6` (W-Q7). A manifest holds one value, so the dark `#1F2322` is the app's `<meta name="theme-color" media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)">`. The snippet is `sources/logo/manifest-snippet.json`.

**Pictures.** Catalogue pictures sit on white tiles in light. In dark the tile is `#E4E6E2` and the picture is multiplied onto it (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`), so its white ground takes the tile's colour (Ulf, dark round: "B"). The user's own photos (a copy's photos, Part Spotter's "Your photo") are never blended, inverted or filtered. The tile stays as it is for U (#526 round, W-Q5 option (a)); taking the white ground off the pictures (#516, edge matting at fetch time, then tiles matching the page) is a later option in milestone T, with Ulf's approval.

SUCCESS: Say (violet means found or act), Unify (one role, one token, the same names light and dark), Check (every pair's contrast recorded and passing), Express (colour for meaning only).

## 4. Type

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-03): IBM Plex Sans for text, IBM Plex Sans Condensed in capitals for tape, tabs and small section labels only, IBM Plex Mono for numbers, codes and set numbers (tabular). Set names and headings are sentence case. The files and their licence are in `fonts/` (`fonts/README.md`). The app serves IBM Plex since #492 part 1 (PR #539); Inter and Geist Mono remain only on the printed labels and the website, and Patrick Hand only on the website. SUCCESS: Unify, Structure.

## 5. Layout

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-01): four tabs across the top (Collection, Workbench, Planning, Manage), the open group's pages in a row under them; on the phone the same four groups in the tab bar and the group's pages as a scrolling row, 40 px high on the phone (content height, as shipped and measured). The lockup at 32 px leads the app bar. Every page opens with its group and page in small capitals above the heading ("Workbench · Orders"). Radii 3, 4 and 8 px; 1 px hairlines and a 1.5 px ink rule under headers, tiles and the app bar; 44 px targets, 52 to 56 px for the main phone action. The phone app bar's icons sit 1.5 px off the frame's position (accepted). Desktop puts a list beside its detail where the decision needs both (Build Scout, Storage, Orders, Order missing parts); the phone shows one step at a time and puts sticky bars above the tab bar, never over it. Frames: `screens-b/`. SUCCESS: Condense, Structure.

## 6. Components

Approved for B′ (round 2, R2-04; round 6): primary violet and secondary ink-outline buttons, the ink focus ring (2 px, 2 px offset), tabs, label tape, tags and dashed tags, the X chip for "not checked" (the logo's X on a violet square, white in light and ink in dark; a thin line X without a square means close or stop), progress per copy to scale, one cell per part, fields (sheet: `screens-b/00-components/`); the switch, the checkbox, the source choice, the hatched status tag ("Incomplete", "3 short"), the part colour chip, the bottom sheet and the chart bars (sheet: `screens-b/00b-components-2/`). A choice among a few (segments: Drawers/Ledger, Copy 1/Copy 2, All/BrickLink/Pick a Brick) shows the chosen one as filled ink (the source choice of two on Order missing parts is the exception: chosen in soft violet, R6-01); a row of pages (the sub row, the top tabs) shows the open one as ink text with a violet underline and no fill. A field in a form or dialog is 44 px high. The stepper (− count +) and the colour block in the part's own colour come from Set Inspector (round 3). SUCCESS: Unify, Express, Simplify.

## 8. Icons

The UI icons keep the shipped set (1.6 px stroke, round caps and joins, 24 px grid) in `components/icons/` (served from `static/brickwork/icons-ui/`, generated by `scripts/brand_assets.py`); B′ adds no icon and changes none: the phone tab bar uses the shipped group icons (`sets` Collection, `tools` Workbench, `price` Planning, `manage` Manage); the frames draw stand-ins of the same weight there, and the app keeps its own files. An icon takes the text colour of what it sits in and never carries a status or a meaning of its own; the X chip is not an icon but the logo's X. SUCCESS: Unify, Simplify.

## 9. Wording

- **Voice:** English, short, sentence case, bare counts, mid-dots, no exclamation marks, no superlatives, no "private" (say "free and open source"); capitals only where they mimic labels (tape, tabs, small section labels).
- **Category line:** "Free, self-hosted app for your brick collection and its missing parts." under the lockup on the README, as the site's first sentence, on the social preview and at the start of the Unraid overview; the tagline "Don't just manage your collection. Work with it." stays (Ulf to brick-work-marketing, 19.11.: "A: missing parts (Recommended)").
- **Names:** Collection, Workbench, Planning, Manage; Part Spotter, Set Inspector, Lot Sorter, Part Runner, Matchmaker, Build Scout, Orders, Storage; Storage index (the storage places beside Sets); Drawers and Ledger (the Sets view switch); "Copy 1", "Copy 2" for copies; "Official instructions" and "Rebrickable" for the instruction links. The name is "Brick Work" in running text; lowercase "brick work" only in the logo. Never the word "LEGO", except in the trademark disclaimer "Not affiliated with the LEGO Group."; in running text say "brick collection", never "LEGO" as a noun. Exception, the question pages (#544): "LEGO®" as an adjective in titles, H1s, descriptions and text, with ® on every use, never in a URL, the long footer disclaimer on the page; "the LEGO Group" as in the disclaimer. Everywhere else "brick collection".
- **Fixed lines:** the AI disclosure "Built with Claude Code; I review it and use it daily." and the trademark disclaimer above, word for word wherever they appear. The site footer uses the long disclaimer (website exception 2), word for word as the footer of `scripts/build_site.py` carries it, starting "Brick Work is an independent project and is not affiliated with the LEGO Group".
- **Numbers:** no minus before a number ("245 parts missing", not "−245 parts"); nothing counted is "0 of 0" ("no figures"); money currency first, unspaced (`€8.53`), with its age.
- **Empty states and errors:** say what is empty, what still works and one way out; errors in words, with "Nothing was changed" when nothing was.

SUCCESS: Say, Simplify.

## 10. Data and charts

The IBCS notation of the design builder's `base/charts.md`, on B′: bars start at zero on one shared scale and are drawn in ink (`--tw-text`: 14.4:1 on the light ground, 14.1:1 on the dark); violet only where a bar means found or complete; a status breakdown uses the status shapes (complete violet, incomplete hatched, not checked dashed); a long tail folds into one outlined row; titles state the takeaway from the data ("3 of 8 copies are incomplete"), neutral when there is none. The charts the app draws: Statistics (completeness, theme, storage, place), the per-copy and coverage bars, Set detail's price history (an ink line with its high and low) and range bar (ink band, tick at the average). B′ has no good or bad colours; a variance is a word and a sign-free number. Approval: round 5 (ink bars, "Approve list, draw round 6"), the sheet in step 7. SUCCESS: Check, Express, Condense.
