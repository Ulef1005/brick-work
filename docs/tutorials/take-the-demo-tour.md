# Take the demo tour

Do this before you install anything. [The demo](https://brick-work.app/demo/) is the real app on invented data: seven real sets, loose parts, a flea-market bag and a few orders. Nothing you press is saved. It takes about five minutes.

On the demo's Sets page the line under the top bar links the four screens this tour visits: Set Inspector, Build Scout, Lot Sorter and Order missing parts.

## 1. Look at the collection

1. Open [the Sets list](https://brick-work.app/demo/collection/sets/). The headline says how many of the seven sets are incomplete ("3 of 7 sets incomplete"), and each set shows its picture, its storage and how much is missing.
2. Open a set. Its page lists the parts, what is missing and what the copy is worth.

## 2. Check a set

1. Open [Set Inspector](https://brick-work.app/demo/workbench/check-parts/), pick a copy and answer a few parts.
2. The page keeps the count of found and missing parts. In the demo your answers are shown and not saved.

## 3. Find what your loose parts build

1. Open [Build Scout](https://brick-work.app/demo/workbench/build-scout/) and press **Scan**. It ranks the sets by how much of each your loose parts cover, such as **498 of 598**.
2. Select a set. The panel lists the parts you still need, and a price for the lines that have one.

## 4. Find the sets in a bulk lot

1. Open [the flea-market lot](https://brick-work.app/demo/collection/lots/9637c558-464c-59a7-a79b-e1b57f4aff0c/_detect-1/). It is the one lot in [Lot Sorter](https://brick-work.app/demo/workbench/lot-sorter/).
2. **Set detection** names the candidate sets for the parts in the bag and scores them. Open a **Pull list** to see what you would take out of the lot for one set.

## 5. Order what is missing

1. Open [Order missing parts](https://brick-work.app/demo/planning/order-missing-parts/). It adds up the gaps of three sets into one list.
2. The list can go to BrickLink as a wanted list or to Pick a Brick as a CSV file. The demo shows the list; the download needs an install.
