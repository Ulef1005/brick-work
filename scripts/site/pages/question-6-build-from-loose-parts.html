Which LEGO® sets can my loose parts build?

There are two questions here, and both need a list of the loose parts you hold. Recording your loose parts is the real first step.

The answer

  1. 1
    Sets you do not ownWhich official sets do my parts already mostly make? Compare your list with each set's parts list. Count only the exact part in the exact colour, and the minifigures as whole figures. A set at 90 % is often a small order away.
  2. 2
    Sets you own but cannot finishWhich of my incomplete sets can my loose parts fill? Compare your loose parts with what each set is missing.
In the demoBuild Scout ranks sets by the loose parts in the drawer. The tutorial shows it step by step.

How Brick Work does it

Both work from the catalogue and the stock on your own install.

  • Build Scout ranks every set in the catalogue by how much of it your loose stock covers, such as "398 of 430 · 93 %". It also shows the missing parts, the missing minifigures and an approximate cost to finish. Narrow it by theme, years, size and which stock counts.
  • A part in another colour is shown as a swap and never counted in the percentage.
  • Build it · make a copy moves the parts you hold into a new copy of the set and records what is still missing on it, with Undo. Missing parts · XML downloads the rest as a BrickLink wanted list.
  • Matchmaker answers the second question. It lists your incomplete copies that your loose stock can advance, such as "covers 7 of 12 missing". Give or Take moves the parts across. See Matchmaker in the demo.
