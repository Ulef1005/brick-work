# Browse your sets

Do this when you want to see which sets are complete, where they sit, and what is still missing from each copy.

Open **Collection → Sets**. The heading answers the first question, for example *3 of 7 sets incomplete*. Under it, the line shows how many copies and how many missing parts are in view, and the basis of the prices.

## Read a set tile

Each set is a tile with its picture, its number, the used average price and its name. Under the name there is **one bar for each copy**, drawn to scale:

- violet is what you found;
- hatched is what is missing;
- dashed is what is still to check.

The text under a bar gives the numbers, for example *#1 365/375 · 10 missing* or *1,426/1,426 · complete*. A set with two copies shows two bars, *#1* and *#2*. A copy you never checked has a dashed bar, the **Not checked** mark and a count such as *0/1,123*. A sealed copy shows the **Sealed** tag and no bar. A copy a Rebrickable pull checked has a line under its bar, **Checked on Rebrickable**. Its Lost Parts are the missing parts, and a copy with none is complete. If you check the copy or mark a part yourself, the line goes. See [Lost Parts from Rebrickable](sync-collection.md#lost-parts-from-rebrickable). On a narrow screen a long line is cut at the edge of the tile; open the set to read it in full.

## Find the sets in a storage

Sets sit under the label of the storage they are in. The label shows the storage code, its name and how many sets it holds. A set whose copies are in two storages sits under the first storage that is set.

On a desktop, the **Storage index** on the left lists every storage that holds a copy, with the number of copies in it. Choose one to show only its sets. **Every storage** shows all of them, and **No storage** shows the copies without a place. On a phone the index is the **Storage** row above the sets.

When no copy has a storage, the label and the index do not appear and the sets sit in one grid.

## Filter, search and sort

- The tabs **All**, **Incomplete**, **Not checked**, **Complete** and **Sealed** filter by status. Each shows how many sets it holds.
- Search by set number or name. Choose **Sort** to order by number, name, year, missing parts, used price or date added. On a phone, search and **Sort** are at the top of the Filter sheet.
- **Filter** opens a sheet with the other filters, the grouping (storage by default) and **Select mode**. See [Give many things a place](storage.md#give-many-things-a-place) for what Select mode does.

## Drawers and Ledger

**Drawers** shows the sets as tiles under their labels. **Ledger** shows the same sets as one row each. Your choice stays while the tab is open. The other lists under **Collection** (Minifigures, Parts, Instructions, Boxes) have the same switch: **Drawers** for tiles, **Ledger** for rows.
