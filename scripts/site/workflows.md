# Workflows

Brick Work groups its screens by what you are doing: **Collection** (browse and edit what you own), **Workbench** (work on the pile), **Planning** (decide what to buy and finish) and **Manage** (put things in and keep it healthy). These are the Workbench flows and the planning screens that finish sets.

## Check Set Parts

Check one copy of a set against its inventory.

1. Open the set and choose **Check this copy**; with several copies, pick one first.
2. Go through the parts. The header shows the set, its name and progress as a count.
3. Record what is missing. Ordering the list **Largest first** puts a baseplate before a 2 x 4 brick before a 1 x 1 plate, which is the order you handle them.
4. The copy keeps its status, and every change has an Undo link.

## Part Picker

Pull the missing parts of many sets in one pass.

1. Tick the sets you want to finish. The list shows 15 sets a page, and ticked sets on other pages stay chosen.
2. Brick Work lists the missing parts that your loose stock holds.
3. Walk your drawers in your own sort order and pick.

## Matcher

See which incomplete copies your loose stock can finish. Use it before you buy: if the stock already completes a set, the order is not needed.

## Orders

Bring a parts order in and put it into stock.

1. **Import** from your BrickLink account (needs your BrickLink key), paste the store's confirmation email, or upload a BrickLink XML file.
2. A line that is already imported is never added twice.
3. Count the parts as they arrive, then allocate them to the sets that need them.

## Prices, Wishlist and Complete sets

Planning screens for the buying decision. A set's part-out value shows when at least 90% of its lines have a price; otherwise it says "Not enough prices yet" and offers **Fetch part prices**, which queues a market price refresh. The Wishlist keeps your lists as chips with counts and exports to CSV.

## Every change can be undone

Each write records an event and, where supported, an Undo link. The same applies to changes made through the [API](../api/) or an assistant over [MCP](../mcp/).
