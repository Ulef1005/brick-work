12kYour max price with a currency (4a, 7b)
The max price carries its own currency, default the app currency. Display prints the symbol of the max as saved ("$550.00"). Under max gets a filter row and a sort option. When the market price and the max are in different currencies there is no verdict: never mix currencies.
