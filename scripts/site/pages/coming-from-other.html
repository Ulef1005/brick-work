Coming from another app.

Many collection apps export one of two formats that Brick Work reads. Check your app's export menu.

BrickLink XMLRebrickable CSV

How to bring it in

  1. 1
    Export a fileBrickLink XML (an inventory or wanted list), or a Rebrickable CSV with the columns set_num, part_num or minifig_num, plus quantity and color_id.
  2. 2
    Choose a fileIt opens Add with File selected. A file can be up to 2 MB, one review shows up to 500 rows.
  3. 3
    ReviewLines Brick Work cannot match are listed and left out. Copies added from a file get no imported from tag.
