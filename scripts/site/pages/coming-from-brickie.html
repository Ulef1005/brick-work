Coming from Brickie.

Brickie, the Brickset companion for iPhone, was removed from the App Store. It saved the sets you own and want to your Brickset account, so they are still there, and Brick Work can bring them over.

SetsWanted setsMinifigures by CSVPreview before anything is added

How to bring it in

  1. 1
    Check your Brickset accountThe sets you owned and wanted in Brickie are under My Sets on brickset.com, with the account you signed in with in the app.
  2. 2
    Sync your accountCollection sync reads your owned and wanted sets with a Brickset API key, row by row in a preview.
  3. 3
    Or use the CSV filesExport My Sets from Brickset and choose a CSV file; the minifigure CSV brings the figures you own loose.
  4. 4
    Check what came inIn your collection counts the copies, loose entries and wishes that came in.

What comes overCollection sync brings owned and wanted sets with their counts. Your Brickset notes come over only with the CSV file; ratings stay on Brickset.
